What We Bring to
Every Partnership.

Stadium 5G
Full 5G Ultra coverage across every partner venue. Fans stay connected, content creators broadcast instantly, and operations run seamlessly.
Global Brand Reach
Orbiqa's presence in 190 countries means every partnership carries worldwide visibility — from pitch-side boards to broadcast overlays.

Orbiqa creates exclusive digital fan experiences — live stats, AR overlays, multi-angle replays — all delivered over our network in real time.
