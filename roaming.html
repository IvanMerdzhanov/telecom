TRAVEL TIPS

Travel Smart

Turn Off Auto-Updates
Prevent apps from downloading large updates on mobile data. Set to WiFi-only in your phone settings before you travel.
Use WiFi Calling
Enable WiFi calling on your device. Call and text your home country numbers for free when connected to any WiFi hotspot.
Download Maps Offline
Download your destination maps in Google Maps or Apple Maps before you travel. Navigate without using any data.
Enable Roaming in App
Turn on data roaming in the MyOrbiqa app before you depart. It takes one tap and ensures instant connectivity on arrival.

Day Pass gives you 5 GB of full-speed data plus unlimited calls and texts in your destination country for 24 hours. It starts when you first use data after landing and auto-charges each day you use your phone. Days you don't use your phone, you're not charged.
