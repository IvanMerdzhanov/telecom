FAQ

Common Questions

Go to my.orbiqa.com and click "Forgot Password". Enter your email address and we'll send a reset link within 60 seconds. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or call us on 0800 ORBIQA. You can also reset your password in the MyOrbiqa app under Account → Security.
To port your number away from Orbiqa, text PAC to 65075. You'll receive your PAC code within 60 seconds, free of charge. The PAC code is valid for 30 days. Give it to your new provider and they'll handle the transfer. Your number will move within 1 working day.
eSIM (Embedded SIM) is a digital SIM built into your phone — no physical SIM card needed. It lets you activate a plan instantly via QR code. Most phones from 2018 onwards support eSIM, including all iPhones from XS, Samsung Galaxy S20+, Google Pixel 3+, and many others. Check Settings → Cellular/Mobile Data to see if your phone shows "Add eSIM" option.
View your current and past bills in the MyOrbiqa app under Billing, or at my.orbiqa.com. Bills are also emailed to your registered address each month. You can download PDF copies going back 24 months. If you have questions about a charge, live chat is the fastest way to get an answer.
You can cancel with 30 days' notice at any time via the app (Account → Plan → Cancel Plan), by calling 0800 ORBIQA, or by live chat. There are no early termination fees — Orbiqa has no contracts. If you're within your first 30 days, you're entitled to a full refund of your first month's payment.
