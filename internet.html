FAQ

Fiber Questions Answered

10 Gbps is 10,000 Megabits per second — fast enough to download a 4K movie in under 4 seconds, run 1,000 simultaneous HD video calls, or back up an entire laptop to the cloud in minutes. Even in a household with 50 devices streaming simultaneously, you'll never notice a slowdown.
Yes, you'll need a router to connect your devices. We recommend the SmartHub Pro ($299) or SmartHub Max ($399), both available outright or on monthly payments. If you prefer to use your own hardware, we'll provide the ONT (optical network terminal) and you connect to it directly.
A standard installation takes 2–4 hours from start to finish. Our engineer runs the fiber cable from the street to your home, installs the ONT, and connects and tests every device you want online.
If your email is through Gmail, Outlook, or another third-party provider, it won't be affected at all. If you use an @yourprovider.net email from your old ISP, we recommend migrating to a Gmail or Outlook account before switching — we'll guide you through it free of charge.
No contracts are required. All Orbiqa Fiber plans are rolling monthly. Cancel with 30 days' notice at any time. If you switch away within the first 30 days, we'll refund your first month's payment in full.
