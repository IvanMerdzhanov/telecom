FAQ

Bundle Questions Answered

Each bundle is pre-configured for maximum value and simplicity. However, you can add optional extras — such as additional mobile lines, extra cloud DVR storage, or international roaming passes — to any bundle. If you need a truly custom configuration, contact our sales team.
You can upgrade your bundle at any time with zero fees. Your new bundle pricing kicks in at the start of your next billing cycle, and you'll receive a pro-rated credit for any remaining days on your current plan. Upgrades can be done in the MyOrbiqa app or via live chat.
No contracts. Orbiqa bundles are fully flexible — cancel anytime with 30 days' notice and no termination fees. If you cancel within your first 30 days, you're entitled to a full refund. We believe in earning your business every month, not locking you in.
Yes. All bundles support up to 10 mobile lines under one account. Additional lines receive the same data allowance and speed tier as the primary line. Family and household add-ons are priced at a discount — Signal extra lines from $18/mo, Orbit from $29/mo, Apex from $45/mo.
Existing Orbiqa customers can transition to a bundle at any time and immediately receive the bundle discount. Your current billing date is preserved and the discount applies from the next cycle. Contact our team or use the MyOrbiqa app to bundle up — existing loyalty credits are also honoured.
